Privacy and Health Connect

Porki only reads the following data from Health Connect:

  • total calories burned and active calories – for the daily energy balance,
  • step count – for the daily and weekly overview,
  • body weight – only if enabled in settings, for days without a manual entry.

The data is stored only locally on this device. The app has no server, does not send data to anyone and writes nothing to Health Connect.

You can revoke access at any time in Settings → Health Connect → App permissions.

Exporting a backup (JSON) is something only you start, from the app settings.
